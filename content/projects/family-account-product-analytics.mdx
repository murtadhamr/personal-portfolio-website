## Overview

Gojek's Family Account enables users to book rides for their relatives or colleagues and stay informed about their loved ones' trips. Positioned as a growth initiative, the product supports both user acquisition and retention.

I led product performance analytics for this feature end to end — from defining success metrics and designing the data pipelines, to building the performance monitoring dashboards. Throughout the project I partnered closely with product and business managers to turn data into decisions.
